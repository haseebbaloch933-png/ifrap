About

A researcher of culture, memory, and belonging

I am a cultural anthropologist whose research examines how communities sustain identity through ritual, storytelling, and material practice. Drawing on participant observation, oral history, and archival work, I trace the ways ordinary life reveals larger structures of power and care.

My approach is collaborative and community-centered: I work with people, not simply about them, and I am committed to research that gives back to the communities that make it possible.
